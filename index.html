About

About & Help

Maluti Play is a streaming platform for Lesotho, run entirely by its administrator. The administrator uploads the movies, series, reels and short videos, chooses what is trending, and approves who can watch. Target users are students, families and curious viewers aged 15 to 45.

The name comes from the Maluti (Maloti) Mountains that cover Lesotho, the Mountain Kingdom. The logo is a view of a winding road through those mountains. Any third-party video is credited on its title page and remains the property of its owner.

Frequently asked questions

Open Account, choose Sign Up and send your application. The administrator reviews it. Once approved you can log in and watch.

Members watch videos and reels, save titles to My List and download videos the administrator allows. Only the administrator can add or remove content and members.

Many video sites do not allow embedding. For those, the administrator's link opens on the original site.

Members, My Lists and titles are stored in this browser using localStorage. Uploaded video files are stored in IndexedDB. Nothing is shared with other devices.
